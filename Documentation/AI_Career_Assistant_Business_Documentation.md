# AI Career Assistant
## Business Documentation

---

## 1. Project Overview

The **AI Career Assistant** is an AI-powered platform designed primarily for university students and recent graduates.

The platform helps users create professional CVs, improve their existing CVs, customize their CVs for specific job opportunities, identify skill gaps, and prepare for job interviews.

Users create a personal career profile containing information such as their education, experience, skills, projects, certifications, languages, and achievements. This information becomes the foundation for the platform's career-related features.

The main feature of the platform is an AI-powered CV generator that transforms the user's information into a structured and professional CV. Users can review and edit the generated CV, select a suitable template, and export the final version as a PDF.

The platform is designed to go beyond simple CV generation. Its long-term goal is to become an **AI Career Assistant** that supports students throughout the job-search and career-preparation process.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Students and recent graduates often face difficulties when preparing for the job market, particularly when creating their first professional CV.

Many students have valuable skills, university projects, certifications, and other experiences but struggle to present them effectively. Students with limited or no formal work experience may also be unsure about what information can be included in a CV.

Common challenges include:

- Lack of experience creating professional CVs.
- Difficulty organizing personal and professional information.
- Difficulty describing university and personal projects professionally.
- Uncertainty about which skills and experiences should be highlighted.
- Using the same CV for different job opportunities.
- Difficulty determining whether a CV meets common recruitment standards.
- Lack of understanding of Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
- Difficulty identifying skills that are missing for a specific position.
- Lack of effective interview preparation.

These challenges can cause students to underestimate or poorly communicate their actual qualifications.

### Business Opportunity

There is an opportunity to provide students with a single platform that helps them build and manage their professional profile and use it to prepare for different career opportunities.

Instead of creating a CV from scratch every time, users can maintain one central career profile and use the platform to generate, analyze, and customize their CV based on their goals.

## 3. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is an **AI-powered career platform** that helps students transform their academic and professional information into an effective CV and prepare for job opportunities.

The user first creates a career profile containing relevant information such as:

### Personal Information

- Full name
- Contact information
- Location
- Professional summary

### Education

- University
- Major
- Degree
- Graduation year
- GPA, if applicable

### Experience

- Internships
- Part-time jobs
- Volunteer work
- Previous employment

### Skills

- Technical skills
- Soft skills
- Programming languages
- Tools and technologies

### Languages

- Languages spoken
- Proficiency levels

### Projects

- University projects
- Personal projects
- Research projects

### Additional Information

- Certifications
- Courses
- Awards
- Achievements
- Activities

Once the profile is created, the user can use it to generate and improve their CV.

---

## 4. Main Objective

The main objective of the AI Career Assistant is:

> **To help students and recent graduates create professional CVs and better prepare themselves for job opportunities through an AI-powered career platform.**

The platform aims to make the career preparation process:

- Easier
- Faster
- More personalized
- More accessible
- More effective

The platform should allow users to focus on their qualifications and career goals rather than spending excessive time worrying about CV structure, wording, and formatting.

---

## 5. Target Users

### 5.1 University Students

The primary target audience is university students, particularly students who are:

- Looking for internships.
- Applying for their first job.
- Searching for training opportunities.
- Preparing for career fairs.
- Applying for scholarships.
- Building their first professional CV.

The platform is especially useful for students who have limited professional experience but have academic projects, skills, certifications, or extracurricular activities.

---

### 5.2 Recent Graduates

Recent graduates can use the platform to create professional CVs and customize them for different job opportunities.

The platform can also help graduates identify areas where their current skills may not match the requirements of their desired positions.

---

## 6. User Journey

The expected user journey is:

**Create Profile → Generate CV → Review → Analyze → Customize for Job → Export**

Additional career preparation features can be used after the core CV creation process.

### Step 1 — Create Career Profile

The user creates a personal career profile and enters their education, skills, experience, projects, languages, and other relevant information.

The user should only need to enter this information once.

---

### Step 2 — Generate CV

The platform uses the user's career profile to generate a professional CV.

The user can select a suitable CV template.

---

### Step 3 — Review and Edit

The user reviews the generated CV and can manually modify any information.

The user remains in control of the final content.

---

### Step 4 — Analyze CV

The platform analyzes the CV and provides feedback about areas that could be improved.

The analysis may include:

- Overall CV score.
- Missing information.
- Weak descriptions.
- Relevant keywords.
- Structure.
- General improvement suggestions.

---

### Step 5 — Add a Job Description

The user can provide a job description for a position they are interested in.

The platform compares the job requirements with the user's career profile.

---

### Step 6 — Generate Job-Specific CV

The platform creates a customized CV that emphasizes the user's most relevant:

- Skills
- Projects
- Experience
- Education
- Qualifications

The goal is to create a CV that is more relevant to the specific opportunity.

---

### Step 7 — Export CV

After reviewing and finalizing the CV, the user can export it as a PDF file.

---

## 7. Core Features

### 7.1 Career Profile

Users can create and maintain a centralized profile containing all their career-related information.

The profile serves as the main source of information for generating and customizing CVs.

---

### 7.2 AI CV Generator

The platform generates a professional CV using the information provided by the user.

The generated CV can include:

- Professional summary
- Education
- Experience
- Skills
- Projects
- Certifications
- Languages
- Achievements

The AI should organize the information appropriately based on the user's background and experience.

---

### 7.3 CV Templates

Users can select from different professional CV designs.

Possible categories include:

- Professional
- Modern
- Minimal
- Academic
- Creative

The user should be able to change the CV design without having to enter their information again.

---

### 7.4 CV Editing

Users can manually edit their generated CV.

The platform should allow users to correct, remove, or add information after the CV has been generated.

---

### 7.5 CV Preview

Users can preview their CV before exporting the final version.

This allows them to verify both the content and appearance of the CV.

---

### 7.6 PDF Export

Users can export their completed CV as a PDF file that can be used for job applications.

---

### 7.7 Job-Specific CV Generation

Users can provide a job description and generate a customized CV based on the requirements of that position.

The platform identifies the most relevant information in the user's profile and emphasizes it in the generated CV.

---

### 7.8 CV Analyzer

The platform evaluates a user's CV and provides an overall score along with recommendations for improvement.

Possible evaluation areas include:

- Content
- Structure
- Relevance
- Skills
- Keywords
- Completeness

---

### 7.9 ATS Optimization

The platform can evaluate whether the CV follows common practices that make it easier for Applicant Tracking Systems to process.

The system can identify potential issues related to:

- Keywords
- Section organization
- Formatting
- Relevance
- Unnecessary information

---

### 7.10 "I Don't Have Experience" Assistant

The platform provides additional guidance for students who have little or no formal work experience.

The system can ask users about:

- University projects
- Personal projects
- Volunteer activities
- Competitions
- Student organizations
- Certifications
- Courses
- Freelance work

This helps students recognize experiences that can demonstrate their abilities even if they have not previously held a formal job.

The platform should help users present their real experiences professionally without inventing qualifications or achievements.

---

### 7.11 Skill Gap Analysis

The platform compares a user's skills with the requirements of a target job.

The result can identify:

- Skills the user already possesses.
- Skills required by the position.
- Skills that may be missing.
- Areas the user could improve.

This feature is intended to help users understand what they may need to develop to become more competitive for a particular position.

---

### 7.12 Interview Preparation

Users can prepare for interviews using their CV and the job description.

The platform can generate relevant interview questions based on:

- The user's background.
- Their experience.
- Their projects.
- Their skills.
- The requirements of the target position.

This feature can be developed further after the core CV functionality has been validated.

---

## 8. Business Value

The platform provides value primarily by helping students and recent graduates create and improve their professional CVs.

It helps users:

- Save time when creating a CV.
- Present their education, skills, projects, and experience professionally.
- Identify information that can strengthen their CV.
- Customize their CV for specific job opportunities.
- Understand how well their CV matches a job description.
- Identify skills that may be missing for a specific position.
- Prepare for interviews based on their background and target position.

The main value of the platform is that it combines **CV creation, CV improvement, and job-specific customization** in one place.

---

## 9. Value Proposition

The main value proposition of the platform is:

> **"Build your professional profile once and use AI to create, improve, and customize your CV for different career opportunities."**

Instead of creating a new CV from scratch for every application, users can maintain one career profile and use it to generate different CV versions based on their goals.

The MVP should include:

3. CV Templates
4. CV Editing
5. CV Preview
6. PDF Export
7. Job-Specific CV Generation
8. Basic CV Analysis

The MVP should answer the primary business question:

> **Can the platform transform a student's information into a professional CV that the student finds useful and is willing to use for real opportunities?**

Features such as advanced skill-gap analysis and interview preparation can be developed after the core product has been validated.

## 15. Non-Technical Product Requirements

The platform should be:

### Easy to Use

Students should be able to create a CV without requiring previous CV-writing knowledge.

### Fast

The CV generation process should require minimal effort from the user.

### Professional

Generated CVs should have professional content, structure, and presentation.

### Personalized

The platform should use the user's individual background rather than producing generic CVs.

### User-Controlled

Users should be able to review and modify AI-generated content before using it.

### Accessible

The platform should be suitable for students with different levels of professional experience.

---

## 16. Risks and Challenges

### AI-Generated Information

AI-generated content may contain inaccurate or exaggerated information.

The platform should ensure that users can review and edit all generated content before using it.

### Lack of Professional Experience

Some students may have very limited information for their CV.

The platform should help users identify relevant academic and personal experiences without inventing qualifications.

### Generic CV Content

AI-generated CVs may become repetitive or generic.

The platform should focus on personalization and information provided by the individual user.

### User Privacy

The platform will handle personal and career-related information.

User data should therefore be handled responsibly and protected appropriately.

### Over-Reliance on AI

The platform should assist users rather than completely replace their judgment.

Users should remain responsible for reviewing the final CV before submitting it.

---

## 17. Conclusion

The AI Career Assistant aims to simplify the transition from university to the professional world by providing students with an AI-powered platform for CV creation and career preparation.

The core product allows users to create a career profile and transform their information into professional CVs. The platform then extends beyond basic CV generation by allowing users to analyze their CVs and customize them for specific job opportunities.

Future features can further support users by helping them understand skill gaps and prepare for interviews.

The long-term goal is to create a platform that supports students throughout their early career journey rather than providing only a one-time CV generation service.

### Core Concept

> **One career profile. Multiple opportunities. AI-powered career preparation.**
